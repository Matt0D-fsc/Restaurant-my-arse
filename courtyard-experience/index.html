EVERY MOMENT IS CONCEIVED AS AN UNHURRIED SANCTUARY, WHERE REIMAGINED GASTRONOMY MEETS NATURAL LUMINESCENCE.

Receiving in serenity has never been so refined.

The Courtyard proposes a contemporary, precise and sensitive cuisine, in the service of rare moments. Here, no standardized formulas: each menu, each service, each detail is adapted to your occasion, to your desires.

Private dinner, confidential celebration, moment of exception with loved ones: fine gastronomy enters your world, without compromise.

AN AUTEUR GASTRONOMY, ANCHORED IN LIVING HERITAGE.

The cuisine of The Courtyard is distinguished by a respectful approach to raw ingredients and a continuous pursuit of harmony. Inspirations are diverse yet meticulously mastered: contemporary French technique, subtle Mediterranean balance, and reimagined Bengali classics.

Every plate tells an unhurried story.

SERVICE BREAKDOWN

Excellence in Four Movements

Every evening at The Courtyard is orchestrated as an unhurried, multisensory journey. From preliminary private consultation to candlelit post-dinner teas, our bespoke care remains effortless and complete.

01 / TASTINGS

Bespoke Curation

Preliminary alignment to tailor multi-course tasting menus suited to your personal preferences, dietary nuances, and seasonal availability.

02 / SOURCING

Artisanal Harvest

Daily hand-selection of indigenous herbs, wild tiger prawns, slow-aged meats, and botanical spices from trusted sustainable growers.

03 / SANCTUARY

Luminous Setting

Natural overhead daylight transitioning into ambient candlelight beneath glass skylights, framed by lush flora and fine contemporary art.

04 / HOSPITALITY

Dedicated Concierge

Discreet sommelier tea pairing, seamless live course pacing, and complete attentiveness allowing you and your guests to share timeless adda.
