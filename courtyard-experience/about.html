OUR GENESIS & PHILOSOPHY

Where architecture, art & gastronomy converge.

CONCEPTION

A Luminescent Oasis in Dhaka

The Courtyard at Park Heights was conceived as a respite from the pulsating energy of Dhaka. Our architectural masterplan incorporates large open spans, natural light channels, clean minimalist geometry, and high-quality organic textures.

It promises to emanate a luminescence like no other eatery in town — welcoming guests into an environment where time slows down for genuine connection and conversation.

PILLAR I

Food with Soul

Honoring Bengali heritage with deep, caramelized flavors alongside contemporary Asian and Continental craft without artificial additives or shortcuts.

PILLAR II

Living Art Sanctuary

Our landscaped glass-covered patio serves as an active sculpture gallery and exhibition platform for both renowned masters and promising local artists.

PILLAR III

The Culture of Adda

Spacious, unhurried seating designed for endless flows of conversation, quiet reading with single-origin coffee, or extended collaborative sessions.
